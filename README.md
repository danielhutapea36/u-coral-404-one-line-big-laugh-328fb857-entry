## The line

> My bank called to congratulate me on saving money, then charged me a fee for the phone call.

The joke is in the turn: the setup sounds like good news (the bank noticed the saving), then the punchline reframes it — the call that delivered the congratulations is itself billable. Everyday, economical, and no stock bank joke.
